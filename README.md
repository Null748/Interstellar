# INTERSTELLAR

A cinematic, interactive experience inspired by the universe of *Interstellar*.

Journey through space and time as you explore the mission, discover distant planets, learn about the science behind the film, and experience the story in an immersive digital environment.

**Features**
- Cinematic opening sequence
- Atmospheric space visuals and animations
- Interactive exploration
- The planets, spacecraft, and crew
- Science behind the movie
- Original soundtrack-inspired atmosphere

*An exploration of space, time, and humanity.*
