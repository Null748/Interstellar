01 — THE BEGINNING

EARTH

There was a time when humanity looked toward the stars and wondered what waited beyond them.

But the world beneath our feet was changing. Dust storms consumed the land. Crops began to fail. The future of humanity was becoming uncertain.

Earth had given us everything. But could it continue to sustain us?

02 — THE UNKNOWN

THE MISSION

Humanity's survival depended on something greater than the world it knew.

Beyond Earth, a mission was already underway. A journey into the unknown, searching for a place where humanity could begin again.

But the distance between worlds was only the beginning of the challenge.

03 — BEYOND OUR GALAXY

THE WORMHOLE

Near Saturn, something extraordinary had appeared. A passage through space itself.

A wormhole — a bridge connecting distant regions of the universe. What would take thousands of years to cross could now become possible.

On the other side was a galaxy filled with possibilities. And unimaginable dangers.

04 — THE FIRST WORLD

MILLER'S PLANET

A world covered in endless water, orbiting dangerously close to an enormous black hole.

Here, time behaved differently. One hour on this planet was equivalent to seven years back on Earth.

Every second mattered. Every decision carried a cost that could not be undone.

05 — THE FROZEN WORLD

MANN'S PLANET

Far from the warmth of Earth was another world. A frozen landscape, seemingly untouched by life.

It was believed to hold the answer to humanity's survival. But the truth was far more complicated than anyone could have imagined.

Sometimes, the greatest danger is not the universe around us. It is ourselves.

06 — BEYOND TIME

THE FIFTH DIMENSION

What if time was not simply something we moved through, but something we could see?

Beyond the familiar dimensions of space and time existed something extraordinary. A place where moments could be explored, connected, and reached in ways we could never have imagined.

Perhaps the universe was never as simple as we believed.
